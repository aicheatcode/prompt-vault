WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy

This page describes how the current downloadable version of the public prompt library works. It does not describe the practices of external websites or your hosting provider.

Information entered on this site

The public library does not include an account registration form. Saved prompt IDs are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not automatically synchronized with Google Sheets, an online account, or another device.

Local storage

Removing site data in your browser can delete saved items. The current website does not provide a JSON export control. Copy any prompt text you wish to retain before clearing browser storage or changing devices.

External services

Links to third-party services open separate websites. Those services may have their own privacy practices. Check their terms before entering personal information. The public website package does not contain a Google account login or a payment form.

Hosting and updates

This website package does not include analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process standard technical requests and logs under its own policies. The public library may retrieve published resource updates if a separately configured feed is enabled.

Questions

For questions about materials hosted by third parties, use the contact details supplied by those publishers. This static website package does not provide a support email address.
