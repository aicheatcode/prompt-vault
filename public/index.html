How to use it

Find. Adapt. Check.

Choose a task, open an instruction, replace placeholders with your own context, and review the output. Results can vary by tool, version and input. The source archive is not a catalogue of independently verified AI results.

Some records are variations of a shared task. Collection counts describe records, not independently authored or tested outcomes.
